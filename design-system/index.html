01

Style analysis

What defines the reference, and the rule each observation becomes.

Frosted glass panels

The window, sidebar and cards are translucent white over a pastel field. Each nested layer is more opaque than its parent, so content gets more legible the closer it is to the user.

rule → 4 opacity steps: 28 / 46 / 64 / 82%

Soft, top-down lighting

There are no hard edges. Every surface has a 1px white top highlight, and its shadows are wide, low-alpha and tinted blue-violet. That light comes from the backdrop color, not from black.

rule → shadow alpha ≤ 0.22, tint rgb(72,88,180)

Pastel blue → purple palette

The canvas runs from sky blue at the top left to lilac at the bottom right. A saturated cobalt is kept for actions only, with violet and coral for small decorative accents.

rule → one action color (#3563E9)

Layered depth

Depth reads in four layers: canvas → window → sidebar/content → card → control. Elevation is shown by opacity, blur and shadow together, never by darkening.

rule → blur ↓ as opacity ↑

Calm composition

The layout is a sidebar plus a single content column. It has generous 24–32px gutters, pill-shaped controls, and one hero banner at the top. Each card holds a single row of actions.

rule → 4px grid, radius 12–28px, pill controls

Modern, creative mood

Glossy 3D primitives (torus, orb, cone, cube) and a mint-to-lavender hero gradient add play. They are confined to the hero and decorative zones.

rule → 3D shapes only in hero / empty states

Guardrails

What this system rejects

✕ Low contrast

Text never sits directly on the canvas or on surfaces below 64% opacity. Ink tokens are tested at ≥ 4.5:1 against white glass.

✕ Harsh shadows

No black and no tight offsets. Every shadow is a two-layer, blue-tinted, negative-spread blur.

✕ Clutter

A max of one primary button per card, at least 16px padding, and dividers at 7% ink rather than boxes inside boxes.

✕ Dark themes

There are no dark surfaces. The darkest fill allowed is the action color; ink is used for text only.
